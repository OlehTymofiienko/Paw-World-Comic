# Paw World — Adventures of Friends / Світ Лапок

An illustrated educational comic for children about friendship, adventures, discovery, time, and the Maya calendar.

Ілюстрований пізнавальний комікс для дітей про дружбу, пригоди, відкриття, час і календар майя.

## Contents / Зміст

- **Pages 1–16:** illustrated story, adventures and educational activities.
- **Page 17:** two Maya calendar interface illustrations arranged on one sheet.
- **Page 18:** a combined final page uniting the original closing scenes.

English lettering and smaller inscriptions in the first translated edition may require further proofreading and layout refinement.
